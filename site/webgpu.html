Your browser needs WebGPU

The Margo Engine demos draw with WebGPU. Update to one of these and reload the demo:

  • Chrome or Edge 113 or newer on Windows, macOS or ChromeOS; Chrome 121 or newer on Android 12 or newer.
  • Safari 26 or newer on macOS, iOS or iPadOS 26.
  • Firefox 141 or newer on Windows.

Still blocked on a current browser? Turn on hardware acceleration in its settings and update the graphics driver: some older GPUs are left out. On Linux, WebGPU may need to be switched on in the browser's flags.
