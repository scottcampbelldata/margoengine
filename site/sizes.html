Loaded later, and what loads it

Every file a package fetches or compiles on its own after the entry you import: the stage's packs, workers and Wasm runtimes. Workers, glue scripts and .wasm files are measured as served, because your bundler copies them as they are. The stage's packs and the BVH module worker are bundled and minified like the entries below (the packs with core left out, since the stage already loaded it). The 3D physics kernel is inlined as a blueprint inside @margoengine/physics, so it makes no separate .wasm request.
