Error help

Margo Engine keeps its error messages short and ends each one with a link to its entry on this page, for example Mesh disposed; see https://margoengine.com/errors#disposed. Errors that are not a TypeError or RangeError are a MargoError with a stable code: branch on error.code, not on the message. Optional packages follow the same pattern.

Codes

  • version
  • disposed
  • unsupported
  • state
  • failed
  • timeout
  • attached

version: incompatible core installations or package builds

Cause. A package and engine resolve different core module paths, or the package was built against a different exact core version. Separate installs have separate class identities and registries even when their version numbers match.

Fix. Use one @margoengine/core installation and rebuild or update the packages to its exact version. Check the versions and module paths in the error. Deduplicate dependencies and map umbrella and scoped imports to the same core. The check runs before plugin setup or runtime access and does not create a global registry.

disposed: used after dispose()

Engine disposed Mesh disposed Transforms disposed Lights disposed; create another addLights handle Viewer extras disposed; create another viewer Pipeline is disposed; create a new graph Pipeline disposed; create a new graph

Cause. The engine, a mesh or the transform store was disposed, and the code kept a reference and called it again. A pick still waiting when the engine is disposed resolves null instead.

Fix. Drop references when you call dispose(), and create a new Engine or mesh (engine.createMesh()) to keep drawing. Check engine.disposed before late callbacks (timers, fetches, promise continuations) touch the engine.

unsupported: the browser or device lacks something needed

Register the viewer element in a browser with Custom Elements

Cause. The browser or GPU lacks a capability the call needs. See the WebGPU entry for the messages core throws.

Fix. Feature-test before calling, or offer a fallback path. Branch on error.code === "unsupported".

state: not valid right now

XR button needs a canvas on the page or the parent option Viewer model has no geometry to frame Physics exists on this stage: pass its options on the first call or set them on the world Clustered already exists; set options on the first addLights call Wait for the viewer element load event or ready promise Viewer extras needs a canvas parent or parent option Hotspot already exists; remove it before adding it again This model has no animation clips Picking disabled; set picking: true Pipeline changed during execution; request the updated output

Cause. The call is valid in general but not at this moment: a re-entrant call from a callback, a missing prerequisite, or a device that is being restored. The re-entrancy, device restore and install entries list the messages.

Fix. Retry later, from outside the callback, or after the prerequisite is in place.

failed: the operation ran and produced no result

BiomeWorld GPU device lost; recreate the WebGPU device

Cause. The GPU or the transform kernel could not complete the work. See the GPU lost and limits entries.

Fix. Read the message for the GPU or kernel detail; most failures are recovered by the engine or need smaller inputs.

timeout: a WebGPU startup step never settled

WebGPU requestAdapter never settled WebGPU requestDevice never settled WebGPU createRenderPipelineAsync never settled Margo: still waiting for <step> after 8 s

Cause. The browser or GPU driver never answered a step of Engine.create: the adapter or device request, or a pipeline compile. Seen on an Intel Arc 140V when 3 or 4 WebGPU pages were alive at once in one browser process: each new page got slower to get a device, and the 4th never did, with no error. The engine warns once after startupWarning ms (default 8000) and rejects after startupTimeout ms (default 30000).

Fix. Start fewer engines at once on one page or in one browser (start the next when the previous one has drawn, or close pages you no longer show). With backend: "auto" and fallback: webglRenderer from @margoengine/webgl2-compat/preview the page draws on WebGL2 instead. Raise startupTimeout for a slow first compile; pass signal to cancel creation yourself. Branch on error.code === "timeout".

attached: the target already has an owner

Cause. Something that takes one owner at a time was attached twice: a canvas presentation owner, a camera projection, a mesh deformer, a sound controller or a voice. The second attach is refused so the first keeps working.

Fix. Release the first owner (call its dispose() or the function it returned) before attaching another, or create a separate target for the second. The message names what is already attached.

@margoengine/physics/gpu-cloth: cloth stepped on the GPU

Cause. GPUCloth was given something it cannot use: a description that is not a SoftBodyDesc, one without triangles (a rope or a bare particle set), a clothTube, a particle with no positive mass or with more than 16 edges, more than 262,144 particles, a setting out of its range (substeps outside 1 to 64, a timestep outside 1/480 to 1/15 s, a negative compliance, damping, friction, thickness or tether slack, a wind that is not three finite numbers), a particle index out of range, a pin point or collider pose that is not finite, a collider shape other than sphere, capsule, box, plane or sdf, an sdf whose data does not match its size, more than 32 colliders, a pin or collider on a removed body (code state), a snapshot of another cloth, a GPUCloth made before await GPUCloth.load() (code state), the CPU kernel cloth-kernel.wasm not served (code failed), or a call after dispose (code disposed).

Fix. Call await GPUCloth.load() once first and serve cloth-kernel.wasm beside the entry, build the cloth with SoftBodyDesc.cloth, clothAnisotropic or trimesh (a tube as a trimesh, or step it on the CPU with SoftBodies), keep each setting within the range the README lists, pin particles 0 to count - 1 at finite points, pass colliders as {shape, radius | halfExtents | halfHeight | sdf, position, rotation, body}, remove a collider or unpin a particle before removing its body, restore only a snapshot this cloth took, and make a new GPUCloth after dispose.

@margoengine/physics/debug-view: the shared physics debug view

Cause. The debug view was given something other than a live Physics world, a name that is empty, a width that is not a positive number of CSS pixels, a source with no debugRender() that is not a function, an engine the world is not attached to, or a name another plugin of the scene already has, or it was used after dispose (code disposed).

Fix. Pass a live Physics, attach it with await PhysicsDebugView.attach(engine, physics) after new Physics(engine), give a positive width or leave it out, add objects with debugRender() or functions returning {vertices, colors}, pass another name for a second view in one scene, and attach a new view after dispose.

@margoengine/physics/soft: ropes, cloth, soft solids and their settings

Cause. A soft-body builder, setting or query was given something it cannot use: a particle, edge, cell or cluster index outside the body, a count, mass, radius, frequency, ratio, strain or pressure that is not a finite number in its range, a material whose moduli or Poisson ratio cannot be represented, a mesh or skin that is not closed triangles where one is needed, arrays of the wrong length, or a call the body cannot take (a gas in a body with cells, a fabric on a body without triangles, a volume on an open surface).

Pass indices from 0 to the count the body reports, finite numbers in SI units within the range the message names (kilograms, metres, Hz, Pa), one value per particle or edge where a list is asked for, and a closed outward-wound mesh for volumes, gas and skins; build the body with the builder the message names.

@margoengine/physics/soft: soft volumes from a mesh (SoftBodyDesc.volume) and their debug lines

Cause. SoftBodyDesc.volume was given no mesh, vertices that are not whole vertices of the stride or not finite, indices that are not triangles or not vertices of the mesh, a mesh that is open or wound inward after welding, or an option out of range (a negative weld, stiffness, damping or radius, a density that is not above 0, a tier other than surface or cells, a cellSize that is not above 0, a material that is not a CellMaterial, or a gas pressure on the solid cells tier, or a membrane without a gas fill on the surface tier or with a shearModulus or thickness that is not above 0 or a burstStretch not above 1, or a vent on a body without a gas, at a particle not in the body, of an area, gas constant or temperature not above 0, a discharge coefficient not in (0, 1], a heat capacity ratio not above 1, or a gas unlike the other vents of the body, or a cue with no time, a time already past, not one action or an action out of range, or a target shape of the wrong length or a frequency above 1 / (2 pi dt)); or a soft debugRender was given a stress range that is not a positive fraction.

Fix. Pass a closed mesh: a @margoengine/geometry primitive {positions, indices}, a core Geometry, or {vertices, indices, stride} with the position first; triangles wound counter-clockwise seen from outside; raise weld (metres) until the seams close, or close the holes; keep stiffness (Hz), damping and radius (metres) 0 or more and density (kg/m^3) above 0; pick tier surface (a gas is a hollow shell) or cells with a positive cellSize and a CellMaterial; give a membrane its pressure on the surface tier, with shearModulus (Pa) and thickness (metres) above 0 and a burstStretch above 1; open a vent on a gas shell, at one of its particles, with a positive area (m²) and a dischargeCoefficient up to 1, the same gas for every vent of a body; give a cue a time from soft.time on and one action, and a target three finite numbers a particle at a frequency the step allows; pass a stressRange such as 0.2.

@margoengine/physics/crater: craters from buried charges

Cause. The crater entry was given something it cannot use: a ground constant outside its range (density, k1, mu, strength, ejected and compaction shares, bulking, blanket or launch), a charge with no energy above 0 J or a negative depth, no gravity, ejecta shells that are not a whole number from 4 to 256, a size or pace factor outside 0.1 to 10, a CraterSolver added without its terrain bed's grid, an ExplosionSolver and CraterSolver in different origin cells (code state), or a crater row that does not exist (code state).
