Orbit a full 360°. Drag the orb with a mouse or one finger; drag elsewhere to orbit. Pinch to zoom. Each slider follows its own arm motion: across the front, down to overhead, or folded through the side and out in front. All three pass Home at 50%.

Focus the scene: arrows orbit, Shift + arrows pan, + / − zoom. Focus the orb button: arrows move it, Page Up / Down change depth. Escape releases a drag.
