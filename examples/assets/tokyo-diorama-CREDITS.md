# Tokyo corner diorama

Original work made for Margo Engine and released under the repository's MIT license.
Generated by `examples/assets/source/tokyo_diorama/build.py` (Blender 5.3); `source/tokyo-diorama.blend` is the saved result.

The genre and presentation (a dense, stylised street-corner diorama with keyframed motion) are inspired by
the three.js `webgl_animation_keyframes` example, which shows *Littlest Tokyo* by Glen Fox (CC-BY 4.0,
https://www.artstation.com/artwork/1AGwX). No geometry, textures, signage, characters or layout from that
work are used.
