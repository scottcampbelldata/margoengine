# Boulder robot

`boulder-robot.glb` is the climber of the Boulder demo: the Light Keeper robot with a hip, two legs (hip, knee, ankle
and toe joints), climbing shoes and two foot target markers. The geometry, the armature, the skin weights, the idle clip
and the flat-colour materials are original work created for Margo Engine, authored in Blender by
`source/skinning_ik/build_robot.py` (`VARIANT = "boulder"`) with no downloaded models, textures or images, and
optimized by `scripts/build-boulder-assets.mjs` (margo-convert, web preset).
