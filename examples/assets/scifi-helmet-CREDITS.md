# SciFi Helmet

`scifi-helmet.glb` is the Khronos [SciFiHelmet](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/SciFiHelmet)
sample model packed into one GLB with 1024x1024 JPEG textures by `node scripts/build-scifi-helmet.mjs`. The source
files are in `khronos/SciFiHelmet/`. Geometry, tangents and materials are unchanged.
