Directions inside a tensor field

Real diffusion-weighted MRI from OpenNeuro ds000030, subject 10159, CC0. A native central 32 by 32 by 8 voxel crop, with all 65 measured volumes.

The tensors use published ordinary linear least squares of log(signal), with a fitted baseline and a signal floor of 1. This is an unconstrained linear fit, with no motion or eddy-current correction. Estimates with non-positive eigenvalues remain in the fit output and are excluded from this display. Glyphs use two-voxel spacing along each axis; all voxels are fitted. Coordinates use millimetres along the supplied FSL voxel-axis basis, with equal physical lengths on all plot axes.

Ellipsoid glyphs

Principal directions set orientation; fractional anisotropy sets colour. Each tensor is normalized to the same largest radius.

Kindlmann superquadrics

Linear, planar and spherical tensor shape controls the superquadric. Colour is absolute principal direction RGB multiplied by fractional anisotropy.

Deterministic fibres and hyperstreamlines

Euler fibres (cyan) and FACT fibres (orange) follow the principal axis with sign continuity. Hyperstreamline cross sections follow the transverse eigenvalues. Default stopping uses FA 0.15 and a 45 degree maximum turn. These are algorithmic paths through the fitted field.
