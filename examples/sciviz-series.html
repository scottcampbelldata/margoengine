Four seconds around GW150914

LIGO Hanford H1 strain, 4096 samples per second. LIGO Scientific Collaboration and Virgo Collaboration, GWOSC, CC BY 4.0. Source, sample bounds and SHA-256.

Each Range request loads part of the recording. The line redraws after each decoded batch. M4 keeps the first, last, minimum and maximum original sample in each pixel column. Min-max and LTTB are alternatives; every method retains the original samples for selection. Drag a box to count all observations inside it, including samples absent from the display line.
