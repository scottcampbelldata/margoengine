North Atlantic wind derivatives

Gradient-based diagnostics of NOAA daily mean near-surface winds, January 1-8, 2024. Q measures rotation relative to strain; lambda-2 is the middle eigenvalue of the strain and rotation tensor squares. A local negative lambda-2 value is a diagnostic, not an observed vortex.

NCEP-NCAR Reanalysis 1 from NOAA PSL, Boulder, Colorado, USA. US-government public domain. Native 2.5 degree samples, 100 W to 20 E, 20 N to 65 N, sigma 0.995. Raw east/north velocity in m/s is differentiated on a fixed Cartesian equirectangular grid centred at 42.5 N. This planar approximation omits spherical metric terms, latitude-dependent grid scale, vertical derivatives and subdaily variability. The singleton vertical axis assumes constant velocity with height; no vertical measurements are invented. Values and sign thresholds are not a full atmospheric vortex identification. Colour spans the displayed diagnostic's min/max each time; sources, hashes and limits.
