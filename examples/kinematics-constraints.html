Positions, controls, solve status and accepted motion stay in this tab. No event objects, uploads, browser storage or automatic downloads. The recorder retains a bounded recent history.

Drag overhead to raise the arm. Pointer and arrow-key requests stop just short of full extension, leaving a small elbow bend. The controller paces large requests before solving. Rejected requests may advance through smaller validated local steps; otherwise the last accepted pose holds. Shift + arrow keys makes smaller moves.

Success certifies static proxy clearance only, not continuous swept motion, mesh clearance or robot safety. The pole speed limit does not bound total elbow velocity. The optional 25° yaw assist belongs to this demo controller; every changed shoulder and link pose is revalidated against the same world-space obstacles.
