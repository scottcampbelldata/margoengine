# Boulder: direction

The page is the wall. The canvas fills the window from the first frame, the opening view looks up from the mat at the
climber mid-reach, and nothing competes with it but one small panel in the lower left.

- One job: choose a hold and watch the limbs find a way there. Every word serves that.
- Headline one word, subtext under 20 words, one primary action (Climb the red route).
- Colour comes from the gym: plywood, chalk, one route red. No gradient, no glass, no purple.
- Type: an old-style serif for the name, a humanist sans for the rest, 16 px or more everywhere, contrast at least 4.5:1.
- The panel folds into a native `details`; phones start folded. Nothing needs hover: the head following the mouse is a
  bonus, and every action is a button or a tap.
- The aesthetic risk: no hero copy over the scene and no chrome across the top; the demo opens as a place.
- Numbers on the page only when measured. There are none in D1; D2 adds the tile and copy.
- The dedication stays hidden: a small brass plaque low on the wall. Tapping it shows the line in the status text.
