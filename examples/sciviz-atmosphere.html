NOAA wind on the globe

Daily NCEP-NCAR Reanalysis 1 east/north winds at sigma 0.995, January 1-8, 2024. Public domain, NOAA PSL. Sigma is a pressure coordinate, not altitude. For this drawing, wind is placed at 20,000 m above the WGS84 ellipsoid; the speed-colour layer at 1,000 m and three reference glyphs at 100,000 m are display placements, not measured atmospheric heights. No vertical motion is inferred. Clear-sky light follows the selected UTC time.
