North Atlantic wind paths

Tubes and ribbons along instantaneous NOAA daily mean near-surface wind streamlines, January 1-8, 2024. Colour and exaggerated radius show measured speed. This horizontal field has zero streamwise curl: ribbons do not spin. These are numerical traces of a daily mean, not observed parcel paths or forecasts.

NCEP-NCAR Reanalysis 1 from NOAA PSL, Boulder, Colorado, USA. US-government public domain. Native 2.5 degree grid, 100 W to 20 E, 20 N to 65 N, 0.995 sigma. The fixed equirectangular projection is centred at 42.5 N and corrects longitude velocity by latitude. Colour uses original measured speed, 0-35 m/s. Radius is the drawing rule 8 km + speed × 1.8 km per m/s, multiplied by drawing scale; full ribbon width is four times that radius. Neither is a physical vortex diameter. Sources, hashes and limits.
