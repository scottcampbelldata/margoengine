# Underwater view

List 3 batch 66 job 5 adds `@margoengine/water/underwater`. `Underwater.attach(engine, { surface })` (or `new Underwater(options)` inside a PostStack, as an HDR stage) takes a live Water or FFTOcean in the same engine and scene. While the camera is below the surface it adds fog, per-channel colour absorption, a waterline across the camera and sunlit light shafts. Above the surface, and with `enabled: false`, the stage passes the scene through exactly.

How it works. Inside the surface's draw (after caustics) the stage rasterizes the submitted surface three times through the shared SurfaceCapture: a per-pixel view-ray hit (normal and distance), a height and normal field of `shaftExtent` metres around the camera, and a single probe under the camera. The probe defines the local surface plane; each pixel's near-plane point is tested against it, so the waterline is the near plane's intersection with the surface and tilts with the waves. For a submerged pixel the water path runs from the near plane to the nearer of the scene depth and the surface hit (`maxDistance` for empty pixels). The background is attenuated by Beer-Lambert extinction `exp(-(a + b) L)` per channel. In-scatter is marched over the first `shaftDistance` metres with `steps` jittered samples: scattering `b` times sunlight (radiance times intensity) refracted by Snell's law, Schlick Fresnel transmission, a Henyey-Greenstein phase with `anisotropy`, extinction down to the sample, and a focusing factor `1/|J|` from the Jacobian of the refracted ray map over the captured surface field (clamped to 0.1..10 so folds stay finite); ambient light adds isotropic in-scatter attenuated by depth. Past the march a constant source integrates in closed form. A `waterline` pixel band darkens the meniscus.

Defaults: absorption [0.38, 0.1, 0.045] /m (Water's own), scattering [0.02, 0.035, 0.04] /m, anisotropy 0.75, shafts 1, steps 24, shaftDistance 40 m, maxDistance 200 m, shaftExtent 64 m, resolution 128 (copied), waterline 2 px, refractiveIndex 1.333. Ranges are in the TSDoc and docs/limits.md.

Proof (packages/water/test/underwater.browser.mjs, Metal WebGPU on the M6 Mac mini and the compatibility run): recorders before and after the stage compare linear HDR. The centre pixel of a box 8.4 m past the near plane matches `exp(-a L)` per channel within 0.0007 relative (rgba16float bound 0.004); the frame above water and the disabled frame below water are bit-identical to the input; with the eye 2 cm under flat water every row above the independently projected near-plane waterline is identical and every row below it changes; wavy-surface in-scatter is finite, positive, varies across the frame and grows with shafts on; an FFT ocean drives the same stage. CPU tests cover validation, stack ownership and disposal. No timing claim is made.
