# Calm-water planar reflection input

The optional water/reflector entry exports WaterReflection, WaterReflectionOptions and WaterPlanarCapture. It borrows environment/reflector output without importing the environment runtime. Attach a Reflector with captureOnly:true, rotate its local XY plane -pi/2 around X so its +Z normal points upward, and place it at the Water height. Size it to cover the visible water. Attach WaterReflection after Water and supply the Reflector. Reflector captures automatically during engine.start; call reflector.update before a manual engine.render loop. Geometry and custom scene passes still belong to the source.

Environment now exposes textureMatrix, the world-to-clip matrix copied when texture was successfully captured. It is undefined before capture and after restoration, and remains paired with the previous image after an unsuccessful update. This avoids consuming a subsequently mutated camera matrix. Treat the matrix and texture as borrowed read-only resources. captureOnly omits the standalone rectangle while preserving update/camera/clip behavior. It defaults false, preserving existing mirrors and refractors.

The water adapter reads the source each draw. It divides homogeneous clip coordinates, converts to top-left texture UV, and samples linear HDR radiance inside the existing water Fresnel/foam expression. No reflection is added a second time; SSR captures exactly that contribution for replacement. Missing images and projection misses fall back to the existing sky. Defaults: strength 1, distortion 0, roughness 0, color [1,1,1]. Distortion is 0..0.1 UV, roughness 0..1 applies a cross filter over up to eight source texels, strength is 0..1 and linear tint components are 0..100. This is a calm planar approximation; displaced/curved mirrors require a different capture model.

Sources may expose the existing environment passes filter. The adapter filters consuming Water draws out of it, preventing capture feedback, and restores caller state when registrations end. Configure filters before attachment. Water disposal also disposes its adapter; source disposal simply makes the image unavailable. Owned uniforms/fallbacks are released and recreated on device restore. Borrowed captures are never destroyed.

The GPU fixture places a red marker outside the main view and independently projects its mirrored world centre. Red increases at that location on water. It also checks exact unready fallback, matrix/image identity, filter lifecycle and SSR MRT validation. Environment CPU tests prove captureOnly skips the surface pipeline while capture remains active and projection snapshots survive camera mutation. This diagnostic uses the already-attributed NOAA wind bearing to orient scene lighting; it is not an observational reconstruction. Research references and implementation rationale are in docs/clean-room.md.
