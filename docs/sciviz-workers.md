# Scientific readers in workers

Batch 175 job 6 adds the optional `@margoengine/sciviz/workers` entry. `readInWorker(reader, args, options)` accepts the existing argument tuple for `loadNRRD`, `loadNIfTI`, `parseNIfTI`, `parseDICOM`, `loadDICOMSeries`, `dicomSeries`, `parseVtk`, `openZarr`, `openNetCDF` and `openVTK`. It returns a disposable session with `value`, containing metadata, typed samples and promise-returning proxies for lazy methods. Nothing creates a worker at import time; there is no page-thread parsing fallback.

Each session owns one dedicated classic worker, loaded using a bundler-visible URL. Dispose the session after the last read. NetCDF variables, Zarr reads, VTK volume/grid methods and individual VTK XML arrays stay in that worker. Multiple sessions are independent. Keep their number appropriate for the host; this entry does not impose a global pool or own other sessions. Reader defaults, validation, compression support and format limits are unchanged.

Input buffers clone by default. `options.transfer` explicitly moves listed ArrayBuffers, detaching every aliased view at dispatch. Outputs transfer with a deduplicated backing-buffer list. Worker results are first snapshotted so a later lazy read cannot encounter a detached cache; this is one worker-side copy and is not a zero-copy parsing claim. Aliases between typed views survive the snapshot. Custom Zarr store, detached-file resolver and FileSource callbacks execute at their original owner over reverse RPC; their returned buffers are snapshotted before transfer so repeated store reads remain valid. Functions and AbortSignals never enter structured clone directly. Plain-object metadata, including null-prototype dictionaries, retains its shape.

`signal` in transport options or reader arguments cancels the session lifetime. A signal on a subsequent lazy call cancels that entire session as well. Termination interrupts synchronous parsing and prevents a cancelled open from retaining inaccessible reader state. All pending calls settle; callbacks receive cancellation on server disposal. No automatic retry occurs. Disposal is idempotent and supports `using`. Calling the proxied reader's own `dispose` returns a promise and invalidates that reader; session disposal remains necessary to terminate its worker. A transport failure or response deadline closes the session. Default deadline is 30000 ms per operation, finite and positive.

Progress is available through `onProgress`: ordered `start` and `complete` milestones with the operation and unique input/output backing-buffer bytes. These are operation boundaries, not network byte totals or estimates of parsing completion. Lazy reads report their own milestones. Observer exceptions reject and cancel that RPC call. `@margoengine/worker/rpc` now supplies generic per-call progress with optional transferable payloads and channel isolation; progress after abort or settlement is discarded.

The demo is `examples/sciviz-workers.html`. It loads existing data without new downloads: OpenNeuro ds000030 subject 10159 T1w in `head.nrrd` (CC0, `examples/assets/sciviz/head.json`); NIMH's FSL `zstat1.nii.gz` (US government public domain); pydicom `CT_small.dcm` (unrestricted test-data use), with exact sources and hashes in `packages/sciviz/tests/fixtures/NOTICE.md`; and NOAA PSL wind (US government public domain, `examples/assets/sciviz/formats.json`). The Zarr demo exposes the same 200 native wind samples as one uncompressed v2 chunk, retaining every value. The single CT slice is drawn as a 2D heatmap, not an invented volume.

Evidence: `packages/sciviz/tests/workers.test.js` runs the exact shipped classic worker through a Node transport adapter and compares real data to the direct readers, including typed values, lazy hyperslabs, repeated caches, reverse resolvers, explicit transfers, errors, deadlines and cancellation. `packages/worker/tests/rpc.test.js` checks progress order, transfer detachment, channels and observer errors. `packages/sciviz/tests/workers-browser.mjs` renders all seven formats on WebGPU and WebGL2, checks the worker asset request, progress, reverse callback cancellation, pre-abort ownership and one live layer per selection. Load-time recording per format/size and an inflater A/B remain in the deferred speed phase; no inflater rewrite or speed claim is made.

## Research and clean-room decisions

- Matthew McCormick and Paul Elliott, **ITK-Wasm: Universal spatial analysis and visualization** (2024), SciPy proceedings, https://doi.org/10.25080/TCFJ5130, and the authors' documentation https://docs.itk.org/projects/wasm/en/latest/introduction/parts.html. Its asynchronous worker-based image I/O establishes the comparison bar. Margo retains the existing reader implementations and moves their execution and lazy state rather than introducing another format implementation.
- WHATWG contributors, **HTML Living Standard: Safe passing of structured data** (2026), https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/structured-data.html, and **Web workers** (2026), https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/workers.html. Transfer is ownership movement, methods are not cloneable, and terminating a dedicated worker interrupts its tasks. These determine transfer lists, method handles and the cancellation boundary.
- WHATWG contributors, **DOM Living Standard: Aborting ongoing activities** (2026), https://dom.spec.whatwg.org/#aborting-ongoing-activities. Signals are translated into worker-local controllers rather than serialized as live objects; a caller's abort reason settles the calling promise.
- Kitware contributors, **VTK.js overview** (2026 documentation), https://kitware.github.io/vtk-js/docs/. VTK.js describes browser readers and optional ITK-Wasm integration. The job matches off-thread access for the named Margo readers while retaining their exact supported format behavior. No rival or paper companion source was read or copied.
