# Design envelope

Margo is a small WebGPU engine: core plus the stage facade is 22,859 B gzip (`npm run measure:size` prints the exact figure; the build fails at 25,000 bytes; core alone is 21,134 B of its 21,504 B cap; the 2026-10-30 core cleanup, which removed the view target depth copy pass, `mesh.euler` and four small duplicates, took core from 21,176 B to 20,790 B and core + stage from 22,580 B to 22,859 B; storing a caller's 4x view target depth then added 23 B to each, and `Engine.renderTarget` 44 B to core + stage), and every
other capability is an optional package. That size is a decision, and so are the boundaries below. Each one exists
for one of two reasons: it keeps core small and frozen, or it follows the limits WebGPU guarantees on every adapter
(Margo's device requests none above them, so an app that runs on a laptop also runs on a phone). This page lists every
hard limit, where it starts, what you see when you reach it, and the package or tool that takes you past it.

How to read "Starts at":

- **Enforced**: an exact constant in the source (linked), usually with the test that asserts it.
- **Measured**: a committed result file (linked). Unless a row says otherwise: ASUS Zenbook S 14, Intel Core Ultra 7
  258V with an Intel Arc 140V integrated GPU, Windows 11, Edge 155, AC power, GPU lock held.
- **Not measured**: the boundary exists, but nobody has run the case. No number is given.

Every number here has a row in [whitepaper/claims.md](whitepaper/claims.md) (LIM-001 to LIM-166). Past losses and their
fixes are in [whitepaper/perf-history.md](whitepaper/perf-history.md).

| High-to-low baking (`@margoengine/mesh-edit/bake`) | By method: one ray per texel at the texel center, no supersampling; overlapping UVs share texels, first triangle wins; colour reads the nearest texel; the bake runs on the calling thread. | Antialiasing would multiply ray cost, and a bake is an offline step. | Hard detail edges alias at low resolutions; `missed` counts texels whose ray found nothing. | Bake at twice the size and downscale; raise `distance` when `missed` is above zero. |
| Vertex groups (`@margoengine/mesh-edit/weights`) | By method: a brush reaches by straight-line distance, not along the surface; `skinAttributes` keeps at most 8 joints per vertex and drops the weakest; `remap` needs every edit in order. | Straight-line distance needs no surface walk per dab, and 4 influences is what glTF skinning takes. | A brush can touch both sides of a thin part; a vertex with more groups than influences loses the weakest. | Paint with a smaller radius; raise `influences` up to 8. |
| Closest point and frames (`closestPointOnSpline` in `@margoengine/splines/closest`, `SplineFrames` in `@margoengine/splines/frames`) | By method: the search samples the curve (`samples`, default the spline's `divisions`) and refines the best sample only, so passes closer together than one sample can be confused; frames are stored at `samples` steps (default 256) and blended between them; the tangent is a finite difference. | Sampling plus bisection keeps a query at `samples` curve evaluations with no per-curve setup. | A tight spiral can return a nearby pass instead of the nearest; frames drift from true rotation-minimising by less than the step size. | Raise `samples`. |
| Knot paths (`KnotPath` in `@margoengine/splines/knots`) | Enforced: 2 to 1,000,000 knots (3 minimum when closed); every knot carries the same number of data values. | Place and deform along splines (`@margoengine/splines/mesh`) | Enforced: at most 1,000,000 instances. | Fitting (`fitSpline` in `@margoengine/splines/fit`) | Enforced: 2 to 10,000,000 points, a `tolerance` above 0. | GPU BVH traversal (`GPUMeshBVH` in `@margoengine/bvh/gpu`) | Enforced: the tree, the rays and the hits each fit `maxStorageBufferBindingSize`, a tree at most 64 deep, `far` finite. | Scene BVH (`SceneBVH` in `@margoengine/bvh/scene`) | Enforced: `rebuildThreshold` at least 1; a mesh under active GPU deformation throws, as `intersectEngine` does. | Point and sprite picking (`PointRaycaster` in `@margoengine/bvh/points`) | Enforced: `threshold` finite and at least 0. | Mesh distance fields (`bakeSDF` in `@margoengine/bvh/sdf`) | Enforced: 2 to 2048 samples per axis, a finite box with thickness, and a texture within `maxTextureDimension3D`. | Inside test and mesh distance (`@margoengine/bvh/inside`) | Enforced: `accuracy` above 0, `maxDistance` and `minDistance` at least 0, a 16 number matrix. By method: the winding number is an estimate at `accuracy` below `Infinity`, measured under 0.03 at the default; `pointInside` on a large hole or a surface with no inside answers by a one half cut; `meshDistance` measures surfaces, so a mesh inside another answers the gap, not 0, and a matrix with shear keeps its answer exact but loosens its box tests. | Inside answers survive small holes and flipped patches; clearance answers come with both closest points. | Pass `accuracy: Infinity` for the exact sum; combine `meshDistance` with `pointInside` to detect a mesh held wholly inside another. |
| GPU build for deforming meshes (`GPUDynamicBVH` in `@margoengine/bvh/gpu-build`) | Enforced: the sort keys, tree and indices each fit `maxStorageBufferBindingSize`; indices fixed at creation; the vertex buffer holds every index. | Alpha-masked picking (`AlphaRaycaster` in `@margoengine/bvh/alpha`) | Enforced: mask bytes match `width * height * 4`, `cutoff` from 0 to 1, `wrap` of `repeat` or `clamp`. | Leak alerts (`@margoengine/stats/leaks`, 2026-11-06) | Sees what `GPUMemory` sees: objects made after its attach, sized from their descriptors, and a buffer dropped without `destroy()` counts as live. Needs one full window before it can alert, four minutes by default. Growth slower than `minBytes` or `minCount` per window is not reported, and a cycle longer than a quarter of the window can look like growth. After a device loss the old objects stay counted, so make a new `GPUMemory` and `LeakWatch` for the new device. Follows 64 numbers by default; labels past that are not followed, the totals are. |
| Field telemetry (`@margoengine/stats/beacon`, 2026-11-06) | Percentiles come from 256 logarithmic bins from 0.1 ms to 10 s, so each reads at most 4.6% above the true value; `max` is exact. Frames under 0.1 ms or over 10 s count in the end bins. A failed send is counted, not retried, and with `sendBeacon` or a `no-cors` fetch the endpoint's reply cannot be read, so `sent` means handed to the browser. A report under `minFrames` when the page closes is lost. `sendBeacon` sends the page's cookies for its endpoint; the `fetch` fallback omits them. Asking users for consent is the app's job. |
