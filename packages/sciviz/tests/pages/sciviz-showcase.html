Volume test page: Medical Volume Suite

Interactive intensity workstation for CT and MRI neuroimaging. Ingests NIfTI (.nii.gz) and DICOM series directly with intensity transfer functions, multi-planar reconstruction (MPR) orthogonal slices, real-time 3D cutaways, and GPU marching-cubes isosurface extraction.

  • Real NIfTI fMRI & DICOM slice ingestion
  • Transfer functions: Brain MRI, Bone CT, Angiography, PET
  • Axial (Z), Coronal (Y), and Sagittal (X) MPR slices
  • Real-time GPU marching-cubes isosurface extraction
  • Composite ray-marching & Maximum Intensity Projection (MIP)
