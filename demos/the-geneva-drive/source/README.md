# The Geneva Drive

Scrub the crank or play one revolution. This four-slot external Geneva crosses a 90° interval of input travel while the roller is in a slot. The wheel rotates 90° in the opposite direction over that interval. It dwells for the other 270° of input travel. On the exhibit's dial the roller enters at 150° and leaves at 240°.

The illustrated input and output have a 320 mm center distance. The crank radius is the center distance times sin(45°), giving tangential entry and exit. During engagement the active slot is oriented using the vector from the output shaft to the drive roller. The plate, roller and locking cam are original Margo Engine geometry. The ideal model describes position. It omits friction, backlash, loads and impact.
