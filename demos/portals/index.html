Two chambers meet behind a single wall.

Watch the bronze instrument and blue tower shift behind the arches. The brass post in front can block the view.

How the openings work

Each arch writes a different stencil value where the camera can see it. Margo renders both 3D chambers in the same scene and draws each one through its matching arch. The two chambers angle toward one another behind the wall.
