# The Sarrus Linkage

A two-family, six-revolute-joint Sarrus mechanism lifts a survey platform. The teal family articulates about parallel axes along Z. The orange family articulates about parallel axes along X. Each rigid arm has two rails and a cross brace and spans 800 mm between its pins.

Each family holds the upper hinge axis parallel to the corresponding lower hinge axis. The displacement along that axis is fixed by the hinge chain. Both families attach to the same platform, so the platform cannot rotate or translate along X or Z. Its motion lies along the vertical intersection of the two motion planes.

With angle α measured between the lower arm and horizontal, the lower arm moves outward by 0.80 m × cos α and up by 0.80 m × sin α. The upper arm cancels that outward displacement and contributes the same rise. The hinge separation is 1.60 m × sin α. The control covers 16° to 73°. The 24 mm fixed reference post passes through a 170 mm diameter opening without contact. It plays no role in the kinematic constraint. All geometry uses metres and ideal pins. The platform is an exhibit concept with no modeled load rating, compliance, joint clearance, or actuation.

## Review

1. Drag **Hinge angle** and watch the teal and orange knees move in perpendicular planes. The deck rises without tilting.
2. Turn on **Top view** and move the slider between its two limits. The green reference stays in the center of the circular opening, and **Sideways drift** stays at 0 mm.
3. Press **Raise and lower** in the field view to see the full range in motion.
