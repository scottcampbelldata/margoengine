How the casting is made

The kernel rounds a solid casting blank, hollows it from the bottom and joins four cylinder bosses before machining the through-bores. Rail, rib, bolt and jacket features are part of the exported solid. The section is a live inspection view. The bore opening is the analytic chord where the plane intersects a bore.
