**web-ifc 0.0.78**, from That Open Company, is the optional browser IFC reading peer of the pinned Apache-2.0 Margo Engine IFC package. The demo serves its WebAssembly as a separate resource and bundles the public JavaScript peer into the live-check worker. The peer loads when a visitor requests a live IDS check.
