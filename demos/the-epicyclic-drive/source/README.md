# The Epicyclic Drive

Hold the ring, carrier or sun, then turn the input through one revolution. When the ring or carrier is held, the sun is the input. With the sun held, the ring is the input. The readouts show angles from the same initial assembly by member. Negative angles indicate motion opposite to the input.

The train has a 21-tooth sun, three 18-tooth planets and a 57-tooth internal ring. All share a 9 mm module and 20° reference pressure angle. The planet center radius is 175.5 mm. The 57-tooth ring equals the sun plus two planets, and the sun-plus-ring count is divisible by three, so the three equally spaced planets all mesh with their neighbors. Each planet spins freely on its carrier pin.

Willis's constraint is Nₛ(ωₛ − ω꜀) + Nᵣ(ωᵣ − ω꜀) = 0. Each planet's spin relative to the carrier follows the external gear pair. The three settings give carrier/sun 7/26 with the ring held, ring/sun −7/19 with the carrier held, and carrier/ring 19/26 with the sun held. These ratios also apply to angle changes from the initial assembly.

The original Margo geometry models the 20° involute flanks, nominal root and tip clearance, and a small tooth-thickness clearance. Root transitions and edge chamfers are illustrative. The exhibit solves unloaded angular kinematics. It omits backlash dynamics, torque, friction, deflection and wear.
