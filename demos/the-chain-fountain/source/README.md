# The Chain Fountain

This exhibit compares the height a moving bead chain reaches above its supply with the drop down to the catch tray. It uses the steady, nearly vertical force balance of Biggins and Warner. The pickup coefficient is the share of upward momentum flux supplied by the contacts that feed the chain into motion. The deposit coefficient is the share carried by tension immediately before the chain reaches the catch tray.

For pickup fraction α, deposit fraction β and drop H, the speed is √(gH/(1−α−β)). The rise is αH/(1−α−β). The drawing separates the two legs with a rounded crest at the computed height. Playback is one-quarter of the model speed. Feed close-up enlarges the hopper and crest. The steady vertical calculation omits the details of how individual beads strike or leave the surfaces.
