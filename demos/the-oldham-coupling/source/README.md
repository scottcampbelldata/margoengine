# The Oldham Coupling

Set the separation between the two parallel shafts, then turn the input or play a revolution. Both shafts and the center disk turn by the same angle. The disk also shifts across their axes. Its opposite faces carry two perpendicular tongues, and each tongue slides in one hub's matching slot. Exploded view separates the pieces along their shaft directions to reveal the interfaces. Use Other side to see the opposite face of the center disk. The cyan guide shows the disk center's orbit in the transverse plane.

Let the transverse input and output centers be (0,0) and (d,0). At input angle θ the first slot has direction u = (cos θ, sin θ). The perpendicular slot has direction (−sin θ, cos θ). Their intersection is d cos θ u, or (d/2 + d cos(2θ)/2, d sin(2θ)/2). The radius is d/2 and the center completes two orbits per shaft revolution. With d = 0 the disk turns about a stationary center.

The nominal shaft offset is adjustable from 0 to 104 mm. The hub radius is 139 mm, with an open-ended 32.8 mm-wide slot. Each tongue is 30 mm wide and 176 mm long. Open ends admit the maximum slide while keeping part of each tongue engaged. This is an unloaded kinematic exhibit. It omits torque, stresses, friction, wear and backlash dynamics. The model and artwork are original Margo Engine geometry.
