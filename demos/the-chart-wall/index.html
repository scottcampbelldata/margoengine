Source and chart definitions

The NASA Exoplanet Archive snapshot groups planets by discovery year and the archive’s recorded discovery method. Its composite table brings together values from multiple references. The orbital comparison uses the published radius and period fields within the labelled window. Values with missing fields or outside that window still count in the chronology.

The snapshot is from 27 November 2026. NASA funds the Exoplanet Archive through its Exoplanet Exploration Program, with Caltech operating the archive under contract.

This record reflects which planets were discovered and catalogued. Different detection methods find planets with different characteristics.
