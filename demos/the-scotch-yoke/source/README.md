# The Scotch Yoke

Scrub the crank from 0° to 360°. The machined red yoke travels on a fixed horizontal axis. Its roller shares the crank pin's horizontal position and moves vertically in the slot. The rod attached to the yoke on the right carries the piston in the cutaway cylinder. Front view makes the two components of the crank's circular motion easy to see.

The crank radius is 142 mm. With the angle measured counterclockwise from the horizontal, piston position relative to mid-stroke is x = r cos θ and roller height is y = r sin θ. The full piston stroke is 284 mm. The playback rotates at a constant 20 rpm, giving simple harmonic motion. The instantaneous piston velocity changes sign at 0° and 180° and its magnitude peaks at 90° and 270°.

The slot has a 42 mm internal width and a 336 mm internal height. The roller is 38 mm in diameter. The straight slot sides extend beyond the peak roller-center height, giving at least 2 mm nominal side clearance through the phase cycle. The positions omit backlash, friction, load and wear. The cutaway, flywheel, pedestal and instrument fixtures are original Margo Engine geometry.
