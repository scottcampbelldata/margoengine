Choose sectors with the buttons, or drag the map. A sector is requested when it enters the camera’s view. Each sector holds ten sample buildings. Fetched tiles can be reused on the return flight.

The district geometry comes from Cesium’s Tileset with request volume city sample, licensed under CC BY 3.0. This exhibit gives the sample a survey presentation and lighting.
