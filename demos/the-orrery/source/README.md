# The Orrery

The date dial spans 1 January 2026 through 1 January 2028. The four positions come from NASA/JPL's published Keplerian elements and their rates. At each date the implementation solves Kepler's equation in radians and rotates the orbit into the J2000 ecliptic frame. The Earth marker uses the Earth–Moon barycenter. Days use UTC calendar dates, which is sufficient for this exhibit.

The mechanism uses 270 mm per astronomical unit in all three position axes. The decorative tracks are projections of the mid-window orbital ellipses on the instrument dial. The bodies are enlarged for readability. Their surfaces are original painted interpretations. The brass arms visualize the positions. They do not simulate a gear train or planetary spin. The exhibit uses an approximate ephemeris and is intended to show relative orbital motion and eccentricity.

For a cross-check against NASA/JPL Horizons DE441, compare Sun-centered J2000 ecliptic vectors at Julian dates 2461041.5, 2461406.5 and 2461771.5. The targets are Mercury (199), Venus (299), the Earth–Moon barycenter (3), and Mars (499). The largest difference over those twelve positions was 0.000336 AU when rounded up.
