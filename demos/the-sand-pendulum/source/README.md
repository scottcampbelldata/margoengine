# The Sand Pendulum

Choose a swing frequency ratio and release the brass hopper. The sand traces the combined motion on the slate tray. The two periods refer to motion across and along the tray. Tray view gives a closer look at the deposited line.

The two small-angle modes use an effective length of 0.68 m and a shorter length determined by the selected frequency ratio. Each mode has gentle damping. The hopper and Y suspension show the motion at their selected lengths. The height variation uses the small-angle vertical rise. Each falling sample inherits horizontal and vertical nozzle velocity and then follows gravity to the tray. The sand line models accumulated deposits with representative width. It omits grain collisions and avalanching.

The scene is built entirely in Margo Engine. Add `?backend=webgl2` to exercise the compatibility renderer.
