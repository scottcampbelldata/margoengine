Model and sources

The disk runs at 0.40 turns per second. The roller radius is 46 mm. At a signed distance r from the middle, each disk turn drives r/46 mm wheel turns. The wheel adds that signed motion over time. The model assumes ideal point contact and ignores slip, torque and inertia.
