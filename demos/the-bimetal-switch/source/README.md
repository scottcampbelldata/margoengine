# The Bimetal Switch

Warm the strip to watch the brass and steel bend together. Once its free end travels through the preset clearance, the strip lifts the lever and opens the contact. The state, lamp and strip-tip reading follow the model. Use Close-up to see the two layers and the contacts at the end of the lever.

The 126 mm laminate is made of equal-thickness layers of steel and brass. Its total thickness is 0.82 mm. With uniform temperature, force and bending-moment balance determine the common curvature of the unconstrained strip. The artwork follows an arc with that curvature. After 3.68 mm of tip motion, an ideal contact lever rotates. The model omits local contact force, thermal lag, snap-action hysteresis and electrical arcing. The starting temperature is 24 °C and the zero-strain reference is 20 °C.
