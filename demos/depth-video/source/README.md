# Depth Video

Play a short original depth-camera capture from the Freiburg desk recording. Drag the view to inspect the unprojected points. The default palette encodes the measured range from the sensor. Source colour uses the matching RGB frames. Both modes share the same 54 depth images and calibrated coordinates.

The source is the [TUM RGB-D SLAM Dataset and Benchmark](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset), `freiburg1_desk`, credited to Jürgen Sturm, Nikolas Engelhard, Felix Endres, Wolfram Burgard and Daniel Cremers. The data are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This exhibit selects 54 matched frames, resamples to 144×108, rounds depth to millimetres and encodes the independently buffered source colour in WebP. It plays the sampled pairs at 15 frames per second. The [original Freiburg desk archive](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/rgbd/dataset/freiburg1/rgbd_dataset_freiburg1_desk.tgz) is linked in the scene data and the [file format documentation](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset/file_formats) explains registration, intrinsics and the 5,000 units per metre conversion. The opening group is loaded before the first working Play action. Remaining depth and optional colour chunks load separately.

Margo's public `DepthVideoCloud` projects each real depth pixel at the registered RGB camera calibration, masks flying pixels and updates the same point GPU allocations as the clip advances. The capture is shown in each frame's camera coordinates. An orbit reveals the raw geometry and the gaps behind photographed surfaces.
