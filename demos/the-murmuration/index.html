The Murmuration

Sixteen thousand starlings above a salt marsh at dusk. Move through the flock and watch the birds part around you.

16,384 starlings

Each bird responds to nearby birds and the disturbance. The flock shape emerges as they move together.

Flocking model

This is a Reynolds-style boid model with a limited neighborhood, inspired by starling roosts. Real starlings have been observed responding to a fixed number of nearest neighbors.

The fixed dusk backdrop was rendered in Margo. The flock and its response to your movement are live.
