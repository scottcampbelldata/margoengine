The Heat Haze

Warm air bends the view just above the asphalt.

Watch the distant lane markings and roadside posts. The heat layer bends rays from objects behind it.

About the effect

A changing temperature field stands in for warm air above the road. Margo bends light through that field. The air temperature difference is an illustrative input to the rendering.
