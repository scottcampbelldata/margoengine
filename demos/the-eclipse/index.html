About the reconstruction

Sun and Moon positions come from NASA JPL Horizons for central Dallas. Coverage is the fraction of the visible solar disc hidden by the Moon. Direct light also accounts for the dimmer solar limb. The horizon is an illustrative clear-sky reconstruction with residual twilight. The corona and skyline are rendered interpretations.
