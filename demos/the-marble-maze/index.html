How the board moves

The whole board tilts up to 11°. A rigid glass sphere rolls and collides with the deck and rails under gravity. The brass frame surrounds a through-hole above the catch tray.

Use the arrow keys as an alternative to dragging. To use motion controls, tap Tilt with phone and hold your phone steady in a comfortable face-up position. Reset returns the marble to the diamond and sets a new neutral position.
