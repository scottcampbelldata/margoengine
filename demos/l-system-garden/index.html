Growth rules

The symbols at generation zero.

Read the symbols

F and G draw a stem. L adds a leaf and B a bloom. Brackets save and restore the branch point.

+ and - turn left or right. & and ^ pitch, \ and / roll, and ! narrows the stem. Numbers in parentheses give meters for a step or radius and degrees for a turn.
