# The Foucault Pendulum

An original limestone rotunda built in Margo Engine. Compare the two direction markers above the stone on the floor, or switch to an overhead view. The brass indicator marks the starting swing. The pale green one follows the current swing. Choose Paris, the equator or Sydney, or set a latitude directly. Scrub elapsed time from 0 to 24 hours, or tap Advance time to show one model hour per second.

The finite length of the 12 m cable governs the bob height. The small-angle swing period is 6.95 seconds under standard gravity. The floor-relative azimuth turns by one sidereal rotation times the sine of latitude. Positive angles turn clockwise when seen from above, with the north as zero. The bob's oscillation remains in real time while the independent elapsed-time control accelerates the small daily change in azimuth for demonstration. The model omits drag, local disturbances, and drive or suspension errors. Geometry and visual styling are original.
