# Third-party notices

**web-ifc 0.0.78**, from That Open Company, is the optional browser IFC reading and writing peer of the pinned Margo Engine IFC package. The demo bundles the public JavaScript peer into the export worker and serves its WebAssembly as a separate resource. The peer loads when a visitor prepares an edited IFC. The application does not modify the peer's source.
