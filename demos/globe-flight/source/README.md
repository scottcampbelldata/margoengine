# Globe Flight

Fly from the North Pacific orbital view to Hawaiʻi Island and descend toward the summit of Mauna Kea. **Orbit**, **Island** and **Summit** move the actual 3D camera. Drag and pinch in either view. The arrow keys turn the current view and Home returns to orbit. Enable the 250 m contour overlay at island or summit scale. Reduced motion skips the moving descent.

Margo's GlobeControls grab the reference ellipsoid in orbit. GeospatialControls fly two geodetic paths from the current pose, and then use the elevation samples to maintain terrain clearance. The Earth-fixed frame has north at +Y and Greenwich at +X. FloatingOrigin shifts the rendered meshes relative to the camera as the view descends. The summit is the measured 3DEP high point in the detail sample at about 19.82061° N and 155.46815° W. Altitude shows the camera's WGS 84 ellipsoid height. The DEM is relative to the local sea-level datum, used here without a local geoid correction. This is a visual exhibit, with a fixed artistic sun direction and atmosphere. [WGS 84, National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency](https://earth-info.nga.mil/?action=wgs84&dir=wgs84). [USGS DEM datum guidance](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-projection-horizontal-datum-vertical-datum-and-resolution-a-usgs-digital-elevation-model). [Jarke J. van Wijk and Wim A. A. Nuij, Smooth and Efficient Zooming and Panning, 2003](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFVIS.2003.1249004).

The same surface shader, globe, grids and imagery render on both Margo's WebGPU and WebGL2 compatibility paths. The full detail set is reused on phone and desktop. The 768 px orbit image opens first. The 2304 px map follows on an idle orbital view or on return to orbit. The two regional grids and the ground textures stream at their route stages. A worker constructs their meshes. The island has 66,049 vertices and the summit has 37,249. The height overlay is generated from those inputs. The browser fetches only first-party gallery assets and does not call a map service.

## Public data

- Reto Stöckli and NASA Earth Observatory, *Blue Marble: Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry*, July 2004, 5400 × 2700. The asset is cropped and encoded in two globe sizes. [NASA source](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/july/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg). [NASA image guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
- USGS 3D Elevation Program, *3DEP Elevation*, National Map. Two Float32 GeoTIFFs are sampled in WGS 84. Their embedded pixel-centre georeferences are in `tools/georeference.json`. The two grids are stored as gzip-packed little-endian signed Int16 metres. [Island export](https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer/exportImage?bbox=-156.22,18.86,-154.76,20.295&bboxSR=4326&size=512,512&imageSR=4326&format=tiff&pixelType=F32&interpolation=RSP_BilinearInterpolation&f=image). [Summit export](https://elevation.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/3DEPElevation/ImageServer/exportImage?bbox=-155.515,19.775,-155.420,19.869&bboxSR=4326&size=512,512&imageSR=4326&format=tiff&pixelType=F32&interpolation=RSP_BilinearInterpolation&f=image).
- USGS, *The National Map Imagery Only*. Public orthophoto composites provide the two land layers. Aerial survey seams and some cloud are visible in the source. The summit map feathers into the wider image. [Imagery service](https://basemap.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSImageryOnly/MapServer). [Island export](https://basemap.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSImageryOnly/MapServer/export?bbox=-156.22,18.8475,-154.76,20.3075&bboxSR=4326&size=1024,1024&imageSR=4326&format=jpg&f=image). [Summit export](https://basemap.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSImageryOnly/MapServer/export?bbox=-155.515,19.7745,-155.420,19.8695&bboxSR=4326&size=1024,1024&imageSR=4326&format=jpg&f=image).

Run `python tools/prepare.py WORLD.jpg ISLAND.tif ISLAND.jpg SUMMIT.tif SUMMIT.jpg` with Pillow and NumPy to regenerate the maps. The showcase build uses [Margo Engine main at the verified source revision](https://github.com/scottcampbelldata/margoengine/tree/0efd9cff8230507a32deb49907ecd499d46925f5).
