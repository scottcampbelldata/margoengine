Shader inputs and supported GLSL

uv is the position across the preview from 0 to 1, with its origin at the lower left. phase is the slider value. Write the pixel to color. Run with the button or Ctrl + Enter (⌘ + Enter on Mac).

This lab accepts straight-line GLSL ES 3.00 with float vectors, local declarations, arithmetic, swizzles and common math functions. Keep the displayed inputs and output when pasting a full fragment, or paste just the statements inside main. Each edit is limited to 36 statements and 800 expression tokens.
