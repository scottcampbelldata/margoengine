# The Resonance Room

Sweep 24–90 Hz or select one of four room modes. The horizontal slice sits at the listening seat’s ear height. The vertical slice runs along the room’s centerline. Blue and coral show opposite signs of pressure during the same reference phase. The sheet is displaced by a few centimetres along its normal to make the pressure lobes visible in relief. The dB reading compares steady-state pressure magnitude at the seat with the largest sampled magnitude in the room. The test-tone button synthesizes a quiet two-second sine wave at the selected frequency.

The 4.8 × 6.2 × 2.8 m room and its listening equipment are original Margo geometry. The acoustic model superposes the cosine eigenmodes of a rectangular rigid-wall enclosure through 160 Hz, driven by a point near the left speaker. Every mode has the same illustrative Q of 24. The spatial modes show the idealized room geometry. The pictured furnishings and acoustic treatments do not enter the calculation. The phase reference tracks the strongest driven mode so dips and lobes remain legible near neighboring resonances. This is a relative model without acoustic calibration.
