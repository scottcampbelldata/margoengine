About the specimen

The model holds the 134 mm bore at 320 °C and the 274 mm outer diameter at 70 °C. The ceramic is treated as uniform with constant thermal conductivity and no heat escaping through either end. The field is sampled at each mesh vertex and interpolated within each tetrahedron.
