Port Meridian is a fictional city. Its solar time follows a late-summer day at 34.6° north.

Model and sources

The skyline, blocks, and façades are built in code. A scheduled lighting wave activates street lamps and occupied rooms. Margo’s screen-space reflections use surfaces and lights visible in the current view.
