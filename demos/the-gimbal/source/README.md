# The Gimbal

Tilt the mount between −44° and +44°. With free pivots the spinning rotor stays aligned with the blue laboratory target. The two rings turn by different amounts to accommodate the tilted mount. The locked comparison clamps both pivots at their neutral calibration, carrying the rotor axis with the mount.

The nested gimbals, bearings, flywheel and plinth are original geometry in Margo Engine. The scale is in metres. The gold dashed guide visualizes the current rotor axis. The blue reticle is fixed in laboratory coordinates.

This is an ideal kinematic comparison. The free spin axis is fixed by torque-free angular momentum conservation in the ideal case. The display omits joint torques, friction, precession and structural loads. The routine maps the fixed room axis into the rotated mount and solves for the two perpendicular pivots. The −44° to +44° mount travel keeps the direction solve away from its pitch singularity. The displayed spin speed is slowed and is independent of the positioning solve.
