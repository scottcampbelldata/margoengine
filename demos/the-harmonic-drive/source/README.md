# The Harmonic Drive

The cutaway shows a strain-wave gear with a 62-tooth fixed circular spline and a 60-tooth flexspline cup. The wave generator is the oval part with two blue index marks. The cup is copper-colored for identification. Its rear flange carries a copper index to make the output motion easy to follow.

## Model

In the wave generator's moving frame, one revolution of the engagement wave relative to the fixed ring crosses 62 fixed-ring teeth. The same contact sequence crosses 60 teeth per relative revolution of the flexspline. If the input angle is θ and the output angle is φ, the constraint is `62 θ = 60 (θ − φ)`. This gives `φ = −θ/30` and −12° per input turn. The flexspline completes a full reverse revolution after 30 input revolutions.

The generator's major and minor axes are shown using an ellipse with semi-axes of 220 and 197 mm at the outer tooth-root contour. The flexible cup transitions from this ellipse at the lip to a circle at the rear hub. Near the two major-axis regions, tooth pitch is compressed by the ratio 60/62, so each flex tooth center tracks a fixed-ring gap as the maximum deformation moves. The tapered angular correction relaxes away from the two regions. The profiles illustrate engagement and clearance with simplified teeth and exaggerated deflection. The exhibit models ideal kinematics and omits stress, contact forces, lubrication, and transmission error.

## Review

1. Activate **Front view**, then move the slider near its left end. The blue marks track the two places where the copper teeth reach the silver ring, while the opposite sides have clearance.
2. Use **Advance 1 turn**. The generator returns to the same orientation and the flexspline reading moves back by another 12°.
3. Turn off **Front view** and drag the slider toward **30 turns**. Watch the rear flange index complete a reverse revolution while the fixed ring stays still.
