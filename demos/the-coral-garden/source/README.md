# The Coral Garden

Advance through four growth stages on a modeled reef. The current slider bends subsequent branches east or west. The view slider orbits the colony. Fish, seagrass and the light on the seabed move when animation is enabled.

The branching colony begins as a holdfast and stalk. A seeded rule gives each child a parent attachment and a time to start growing. Every active segment lengthens and tapers. Margo Marching Cubes extracts a new closed surface around the growing segments. The worker builds each stage from the same seed. The form changes as tips appear. The control is a qualitative model of growth with current, and its stages have no biological time unit.

Three neighboring mature specimens use Margo's procedural coral package. They show tabular, brain and branching forms alongside the changing colony. Real Margo directional shadows anchor the rocks and colonies. The underwater haze and moving seabed light are shader approximations. Desktop and phone use the same geometry, shadow map and material math.

Topology QA verifies one closed component in the four calm stages and both mature current extremes. At maturity the mean x-position of the high branches differs by about 0.96 metres between the two extreme current settings.
