# Inside a Heat Pump

An original procedural cutaway of a reversible, split air-source heat pump. The two heat exchangers, expansion device, compressor, and four-way reversing valve are rendered by Margo with the refrigerant path traced through the model. The Margo exploded-view package moves the covers and exchanger assemblies. The tracing colors show pressure sides of the cycle and illustrate routing.
