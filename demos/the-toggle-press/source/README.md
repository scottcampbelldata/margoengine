# The Toggle Press

The control moves the knee of a symmetric two-link toggle toward the ram axis. Each link is 231 mm between pins. The side screw translates a slotted crosshead horizontally. The knee pin can move vertically in that slot as the links straighten. The guided ram travels downward, and a moving pointer against a fixed scale shows that displacement.

For horizontal knee offset q, the vertical projection of either link is h = √(L² − q²). The distance from the fixed upper pin to the ram pin is 2h. If the input travel increases as q falls, the output displacement increases at 2q/h millimetres per millimetre of knee travel. Ideal virtual work gives the inverse, h/(2q), as the force ratio from the knee to the ram.

The open and closed positions are 52° and 8° from vertical. A fixed stop keeps the mechanism from reaching the straight-line singularity. The display shows the unloaded position and the ideal force ratio at the knee. The simulated handwheel shows the input motion and contributes no additional ratio to the readout. The model omits friction, material deformation, applied load and the screw's torque advantage. The frame, fixtures, dies and artwork are original Margo Engine geometry.
